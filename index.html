About this dashboard. Built from a synthetic Candy AI BizOps dataset: 35 linked tables covering January 2024 to August 2026, with FY2025 and FY2026 budgets and a September–December 2026 forecast. Every chart names the table it comes from. Revenue is net of VAT and reported in dollars; EUR and GBP charges are converted at the month's average rate.

Method. Subscribers are simulated one by one - plan, country, channel, payment method, renewals, churn, win-backs and token purchases - and the tables are aggregated from those events, so they reconcile: the MRR bridge closes to the cent and subscriber counts match the marketing and funnel tables. Lifetime value is cumulative net revenue per original cohort member; payback applies the trailing gross margin to that curve and is a cash measure, because annual plans are billed up front.

Data generated . Charts hand-drawn in SVG - no chart library.
